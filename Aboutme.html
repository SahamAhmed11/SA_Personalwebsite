About Me


Hello, my name is Saham Ahmed. I'm a fourth-year Management Information Systems student at San Diego State University, interested in becoming a Data Analyst. I have worked with tools such as Excel, SQL, Python, and ERP systems. I am currently expanding my knowledge in both Web Development and UX/UI design at my research position in the San Diego State Digital Innovation Lab.

Aside from academics, I love to travel. So far, I have been to New York, Minnesota, London, and Kenya. I plan to visit more countries.

My management internship with the City of San Diego provided me with valuable hands-on experience and a deeper understanding of how local government operations support the community.

Through this opportunity, I strengthened my professional skills while building meaningful connections within the city that I’m proud to call my home.
